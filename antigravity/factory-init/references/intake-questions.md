# Intake questions

Settle each of these, or leave it for later on purpose and say so in `PRODUCT.md`. They are grouped by what they decide; ask the ones that change the most first.

## The problem

- What happens today without this product, and what does it cost the people involved?
- How will you know, three months after launch, that it worked? A number if possible.

## The users

- Who uses it? For each kind of user: what they do, how often, on what device, how comfortable they are with software.
- Who must never see what? (Other customers' data, prices, internal notes.)
- Who pays, if it is not the user?

## The journeys

- For each kind of user, the two or three things they come to do, from start to finish, in their words.
- What starts each journey (an email, a link, opening the app), and where it ends?
- What goes wrong today in each, and what should happen instead?

## Rules of the business

- The rules a person would follow without thinking: who may do what, when something can be undone, limits, deadlines, approvals.
- Numbers: how many users, records, files, how large, how often.
- Money: currencies, taxes, refunds, invoices, if any.

## Around the product

- Systems it must work with (accounting, email, payments, an existing database), and who owns them.
- Laws or contracts that apply: personal data, the country it is stored in, accessibility, industry rules.
- Languages and countries at launch.

## Look and voice

- Is there a brand already (logo, colors, fonts, guidelines), or does it need one?
- Two or three products whose feel you like, and one you do not, and why.
- How the product speaks to users: formal, friendly, terse.

## Constraints

- Budget band, launch date, and what is fixed among scope, date and budget.
- Technology the client already uses, must use, or must not use, and who will run the product after launch.

## The MVP

- If only one journey could ship first, which, and why?
- What can be manual behind the scenes at first (an admin doing by hand what the product will later automate)?
